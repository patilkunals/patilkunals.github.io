# Citi

## Executive Summary

Manager (Tech Lead) at Citicorp Services India Limited from June 2015 to July 2017, leading delivery for mission-critical banking and financial services platforms supporting Citi's global trade finance and regulatory operations.

Led a 20-person engineering team across complex enterprise systems where high availability, security, performance, compliance, and integration reliability were central to business continuity. The role combined technical leadership, delivery governance, stakeholder coordination, estimation, capacity planning, code review, process improvement, and regulatory-driven system delivery.

## Business Context

Citi's global banking environment required resilient enterprise platforms that could support trade finance workflows, importer-to-exporter processing, regulatory compliance, and globally distributed business operations.

The work centered on large-scale financial systems where operational continuity, transaction integrity, secure integration, auditability, and compliance with regional regulatory requirements were critical business priorities.
