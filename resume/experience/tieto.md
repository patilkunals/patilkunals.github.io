# Tieto

## Executive Summary

Lead Software Engineer at Tieto Software Technologies Ltd. from December 2009 to May 2015, delivering enterprise Java/J2EE platforms for Nordic banking and telecom clients.

Built early architecture and technical leadership foundations through solution design, customer requirement translation, enterprise software delivery, application management, incident and problem management, and large-scale release execution. The role established core strengths in banking systems, telecom platforms, enterprise integration, technical design, customer engagement, and disciplined delivery that later evolved into solution and enterprise architecture responsibilities.

## Business Context

Tieto engagements supported banking and telecom clients operating long-running enterprise platforms with complex integrations, customer-facing channels, internal operations workflows, and strict delivery expectations.

The work included wholesale banking agreements, credit card expense management, telecom customer portals, reseller web channels, provisioning systems, and application maintenance across production-facing environments.
