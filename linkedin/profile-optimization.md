# LinkedIn Profile Optimization Guide

**Strategic positioning and keyword-optimized copy for LinkedIn profile based on portfolio documentation.**

---

## Overview

LinkedIn is often the first impression for recruiter/client outreach. This guide provides optimized content for:
1. **Headline** (120 characters) — Immediate value proposition
2. **About** (2600 characters) — Career summary and current focus
3. **Experience** (per section) — Employer and role descriptions
4. **Featured** (media/articles) — Link to portfolio, case studies, articles

**Guiding Principles:**
- Keyword-rich (SEO for "GenAI architect," "enterprise architect," "solution architect")
- Outcome-focused (quantified results, business value)
- Scannable (use line breaks, bullet points)
- Call-to-action (portfolio, email, calendar link)
