# Glossary

Architecture and technology terminology definitions.

## Architecture Terms

- **[Microservices](architecture-terms.md#microservices)** – Small, independently deployable services
- **[API](architecture-terms.md#api)** – Application Programming Interface
- **[DDD](architecture-terms.md#ddd)** – Domain-Driven Design
- **[CQRS](architecture-terms.md#cqrs)** – Command Query Responsibility Segregation
- **[Event Sourcing](architecture-terms.md#event-sourcing)** – Event-based state management

## Cloud Terms

- **[Lambda](cloud-terms.md#lambda)** – AWS serverless compute
- **[VPC](cloud-terms.md#vpc)** – Virtual Private Cloud
- **[S3](cloud-terms.md#s3)** – Simple Storage Service
- **[DynamoDB](cloud-terms.md#dynamodb)** – NoSQL database service
- **[Region](cloud-terms.md#region)** – Geographic area with multiple availability zones

## AI/GenAI Terms

- **[LLM](ai-terms.md#llm)** – Large Language Model
- **[RAG](ai-terms.md#rag)** – Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- **[Embedding](ai-terms.md#embedding)** – Vector representation of text
- **[Prompt](ai-terms.md#prompt)** – Input instruction to an LLM
- **[Token](ai-terms.md#token)** – Unit of text processing

## Banking Terms

- **[KYC](banking-terms.md#kyc)** – Know Your Customer
- **[AML](banking-terms.md#aml)** – Anti-Money Laundering
- **[CASA](banking-terms.md#casa)** – Current Account Saving Account
- **[T24](banking-terms.md#t24)** – Temenos core banking platform
- **[SWIFT](banking-terms.md#swift)** – Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication

## Insurance Terms

- **[Premium](insurance-terms.md#premium)** – Amount paid for insurance coverage
- **[Underwriting](insurance-terms.md#underwriting)** – Risk assessment and policy issuance
- **[Claims](insurance-terms.md#claims)** – Insurance benefit requests
- **[Policy](insurance-terms.md#policy)** – Insurance contract

## Telecom Terms

- **[IVR](telecom-terms.md#ivr)** – Interactive Voice Response
- **[Provisioning](telecom-terms.md#provisioning)** – Service activation
- **[SLA](telecom-terms.md#sla)** – Service Level Agreement
