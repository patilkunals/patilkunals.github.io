## STAR Stories by Topic

### Enterprise Architecture & Leadership

- **Large-Scale Team Leadership** – Leading 20+ person teams at Citi on mission-critical systems
- **Architecture Governance** – Establishing architecture review boards and design patterns
- **Modernization Strategy** – $25M savings through cloud migration and API-led architecture
- **Technology Strategy** – Aligning architecture decisions with business outcomes

### Cloud & Microservices

- **Cloud Migration** – Monolith to microservices, legacy system integration
- **Scalability Under Load** – Handling 10x traffic spikes through caching and optimization
- **Microservices Design** – Service boundaries, inter-service communication, data consistency
- **Performance Optimization** – API latency reduction, database optimization, caching strategies

### GenAI & Agentic AI

- **GenAI Production** – SOP-to-agent transformation with LangChain and AWS Bedrock
- **RAG Implementation** – Knowledge management chatbot using pgvector and semantic search
- **Agent Orchestration** – Multi-step workflows with tool integration and error handling
- **LLM Integration** – AWS Bedrock integration, prompt engineering, cost optimization

### Leadership & Delivery

- **Cross-Functional Coordination** – Managing development, QA, DevOps, business analysis teams
- **Stakeholder Engagement** – Client relationship management, expectation setting, conflict resolution
- **Delivery Under Pressure** – Scope management, escalation handling, risk mitigation
- **Mentoring & Coaching** – Technical skill development, knowledge transfer, career growth

## Common Interview Questions

**"Tell me about your most complex project"**
→ Response: Middleware Modernization ($25M impact, legacy constraints)

**"Describe a time you led a large team"**
→ Response: 20-person team at Citi on TRIMS (trade finance platform)

**"How do you approach architecture decisions?"**
→ Response: Business-first framework with trade-off analysis

**"What's your experience with GenAI?"**
→ Response: Agentic Ops Platform with LangChain, LangGraph, AWS Bedrock

**"Tell me about a technical challenge you solved"**
→ Response: Core banking API latency/rate-limiting workarounds with async patterns

## Interview Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Review target role requirements and responsibilities
- [ ] Prepare 3-5 STAR stories relevant to role
- [ ] Research company and competitive landscape
- [ ] Study technical topics (system design, architecture)
- [ ] Prepare questions to ask interviewers
- [ ] Practice behavioral responses with CAR/STAR format
- [ ] Review portfolio projects and talking points
- [ ] Prepare salary and negotiation ranges
- [ ] Practice under time pressure
