# Executive Interview Playbook

Preparing for VP, C-level, or principal engineer interviews.

## Executive Interview Focus

Not technical details, but:
```
✅ Strategic thinking
✅ Business impact
✅ Leadership approach
✅ Decision-making frameworks
✅ Stakeholder management
✅ Organizational impact
```

## Key Stories to Prepare

### Story 1: Significant Business Value Delivered
```
Setup: "At [Company], the business faced [challenge]"
  Challenge: Specific business problem (cost, speed, growth)
  
Your role: "I was tasked with..."
  Scope: Scale of responsibility
  
Approach: "My strategy was..."
  Decision: Key strategic choice
  Why: Business rationale
  
Results:
  ✅ $25M cost savings
  ✅ Revenue impact: X%
  ✅ Timeline: accelerated by Y
  ✅ Team: grew/optimized by Z
  
Learning: "This taught me..."
```

### Story 2: Navigating Organizational Change
```
Situation: Major transformation (agile, cloud, restructuring)
Challenge: Resistance, complexity, risk
Your approach:
  - How you communicated vision
  - How you managed stakeholders
  - How you built team buy-in
Results:
  - Adoption metrics
  - Team morale impact
  - Business outcomes
```

### Story 3: Difficult Decision with Trade-offs
```
Situation: Multiple stakeholders, competing interests
Decision: What you chose and why
Process: How you made the decision
Communication: How you explained it
Outcome: Results of that decision
Reflection: What you'd do differently
```

### Story 4: Scaling Team/Organization
```
Situation: Growing from X → Y people
Challenges:
  - Culture preservation
  - Knowledge transfer
  - Quality maintenance
Approach:
  - Hiring strategy
  - Development plan
  - Metrics you used
Outcome:
  - Team productivity
  - Retention rates
  - Skill improvement
```

## Executive Discussion Topics

### Topic 1: Technology Strategy

**Likely question:** "How do you approach technology decisions?"

**Your framework:**
```
1. Business requirement first (not tech for tech's sake)
2. Evaluate options:
   - Build vs. Buy vs. Partner
   - In-house vs. Outsourced
   - Short-term vs. long-term
3. Risk assessment
   - Technical risk
   - Organizational risk
   - Market risk
4. Implementation & monitoring
   - KPIs for success
   - Rollback plan
   - Stakeholder alignment
```

### Topic 2: Leading through Uncertainty

**Likely question:** "Tell me about a time you led through uncertainty"

**Your answer framework:**
```
Situation: Unknown territory
  - Market shifting
  - Technology evolving
  - Business pivoting

Your approach:
  ✅ Gather data (don't rush decision)
  ✅ Involve team (get multiple perspectives)
  ✅ Set decision deadline (don't analysis-paralyze)
  ✅ Communicate clearly (transparency about uncertainty)
  ✅ Build in flexibility (keep options open)
  ✅ Monitor & iterate (adjust as you learn)

Result: Navigated successfully, team confidence
```

### Topic 3: Building High-Performance Teams

**Likely question:** "How do you build and develop teams?"

**Your philosophy:**
```
Hiring:
  - Look for growth potential over current skills
  - Diverse perspectives (avoid echo chamber)
  - Culture fit (values, not personality)

Development:
  ✅ Clear career paths
  ✅ Stretch assignments
  ✅ Regular feedback
  ✅ Learning opportunities
  ✅ Promotion/advancement

Retention:
  ✅ Autonomy (trust people)
  ✅ Mastery (growth)
  ✅ Purpose (mission)

Metrics: Retention rate, time-to-competent, satisfaction
```

### Topic 4: Managing Board/Executive Expectations

**Likely question:** "How do you communicate with senior leadership?"

**Your approach:**
```
Understand their priorities:
  - Revenue growth
  - Risk mitigation
  - Cost optimization
  - Market leadership

Your communication:
  ✅ Lead with business impact (not technical detail)
  ✅ Use data (quantify outcomes)
  ✅ Show trade-offs (not just benefits)
  ✅ Recommend (not just report)
  ✅ Regular cadence (no surprises)

Format:
  - Executive summary (1 page)
  - Key metrics (charts)
  - Risks & mitigation
  - Next steps
```

## Portfolio as Evidence

### Prepare narratives from:
- **Digital Insurance:** $25M outcomes, 3-year program, team leadership
- **Middleware Modernization:** $25M savings, strategic cloud decision
- **Agentic Ops:** Current initiative, strategic AI positioning
- **TRIMS, Neo Banking:** Scale, complexity, results

### Quantify everything:
```
Instead of: "We improved performance"
Say: "Reduced response time 10x (2s → 200ms), enabling 5x throughput"

Instead of: "Good team"
Say: "Reduced churn from 18% to 3%, avg tenure 4 years"

Instead of: "Better architecture"
Say: "60% infrastructure cost reduction, 99.95% uptime, deploy daily"
```

## Interview Questions & Answers

### Q: "What's your leadership philosophy?"
```
A: "I believe in servant leadership—empowering teams to succeed.
   Key principles:
   1. Clear vision (why we're doing this)
   2. Autonomy (how is team's decision)
   3. Accountability (results matter)
   4. Growth (development opportunities)
   5. Transparency (over/under-communicate)"
```

### Q: "How do you handle disagreement with leadership?"
```
A: "I believe in healthy debate. My approach:
   1. Understand their perspective fully
   2. Present alternative with data
   3. Acknowledge trade-offs
   4. Defer if they override (then execute fully)
   5. Review results objectively
   6. Adjust if wrong (no ego)"
```

### Q: "What's your biggest leadership failure?"
```
A: "Early in my career, I made [decision] because [reason].
   It didn't work because [learning].
   I learned: [principle].
   Now I [improved approach].
   Example of how this helped later: [application]"
```

## Preparing for Discussion

### Before the interview:
- [ ] Research company strategy (investor presentations, news)
- [ ] Understand role in organizational context
- [ ] Prepare 2-3 strategic questions to ask THEM
- [ ] Quantify all portfolio outcomes
- [ ] Practice talking about failures (shows maturity)

### Strategic questions to ask:
```
"What are the top 3 challenges this organization faces in the next 2 years?"
"How does this role contribute to the strategic vision?"
"What would success look like in year 1? Year 3?"
"What's the relationship between this role and [adjacent team]?"
```
