## Architectural Patterns Used

- **Microservices** — Across all projects
- **API-Led Architecture** — TRIMS, Neo Banking, Middleware Modernization
- **Event-Driven** — Fuel Pay (fraud detection), Agentic Ops (SOP automation)
- **CQRS & Event Sourcing** — Complex transaction systems
- **Saga Pattern** — Multi-step business workflows (loans, insurance)
- **Circuit Breaker & Resilience** — Payment processing, external integrations
- **Strangler Migration** — Middleware Modernization, TRIMS evolution
- **High-Availability & Disaster Recovery** — All projects (99.95%+)
